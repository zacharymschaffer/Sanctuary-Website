Sanctuary

A Healing Home

For Brooklyn neighbors

Rooted in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, Sanctuary is a home for all of us to re-member community through meals, storytelling, art, and mutual care.

Our Story

Founded and hosted in the home of two best friends, we are shaped by Black, Jewish, animist, and other wisdom traditions — and by the belief that we belong to one another and to a living world.
